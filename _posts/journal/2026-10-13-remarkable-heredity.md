One of the remarkable things about the Roman Empire was how well the non-hereditary monarchy worked. The Emperor would just adopt someone as a child who they thought would be a good ruler as an adult. The minute this stopped, chaos.

Which makes it seem that the traits for "who would make a good ruler" are very possible to simply select for.
