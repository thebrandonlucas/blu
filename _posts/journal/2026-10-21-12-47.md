Announcing Lyceum Study!

As opposed to lyceum.quest which focuses on building a comprehensive digital library, this tool is for helping you learn to read Ancient Greek by giving you direct access to the texts and encouraging learning by doing. It includes Lyceum Editions which have curated contextual glosses and verse-by-verse prose translations.

You can do spaced-repetition on both words and verses, doing both together creates stronger mental connections and helps you learn faster and more effectively.

The idea is to give lots of context and minimal distractions for those who want to learn their favorite texts under time constraints.

Your progress is stored locally in your browser so you don't even need to sign in to use it! However, if you want to save & sync across browsers/sites you also have that option.

Try it and let me know what you think!
